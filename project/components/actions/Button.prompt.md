Standard button in 7 variants and 4 sizes. Any button that starts work shows `loading` until the work lands.

```jsx
<Button variant="brand" icon="plus">New trade</Button>
<Button variant="primary" size="large" loading>Approve</Button>
<Button variant="outline" size="xsmall">Review</Button>
<Button variant="critical-ghost">Decline</Button>
```

Hover mixes 10% white into filled buttons and 5% black into light fills; press scales to 0.97; focus is a 4px ring at 25%. Disabled = grouped-light fill, tertiary label.
