Colour

One colour: brand blue #2563EB (--buttons-brand), or white on brand blue. Never a commodity colour, a gradient, black on a photo, or any other blue. The commodities keep their own colours inside the product, so the mark never competes with them.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, outline, shadow or animate the mark.
  • Recolour it, or set it on a commodity colour.
  • Put letters inside it or redraw it. It is one path, from the supplied file.
  • Confuse it with the Lucide link icon. The icon is for links in the interface; the mark is only ever One Link.

Why it looks like this

UAG's business is connection. It stands between Zambia's farmers and its mills, and between grain that arrives by sea and the mills that need it. Every tonne UAG handles is a link between someone who grows or ships it and someone who needs it.

The mark is one link with two loops: one loop is the farmer, the other the mill, joined in a single unbroken shape, because One Link is one record that both sides and everyone at UAG read. The two openings are shaped like grains of seed, carried inside the link, because grain is what travels along it, one load at a time.

It is deliberately bold and simple. It has to be recognisable on a farmer's phone in bright sun, on a clerk's screen at the weighbridge, and as a small icon in the app stores. It is drawn as a single link, not two interlocking chain links, so it can never be mistaken for the link icon used on buttons inside the product.
