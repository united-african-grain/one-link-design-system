The One Link design system

One Link runs a grain and fertilizer business: trades and mill coverage, weighbridge intake, stock, and farmer input finance, with a portal for counterparties and a field app for drivers. Every screen leads with what needs attention, and every figure is derived, never typed.

  • White lifts, grey recedes

    Cards, tickets and figure buttons are white on white, held apart by very soft shadows. Grey marks only what is secondary or switched off.

  • Greys tinted navy, and see-through

    Text and hairlines are navy-black at set opacities, so one token reads right on white and on grey. Each has a solid twin for use over photos.

  • Two layers of colour

    Components use semantic tokens only. Those point at 11-step palettes, so a palette change never touches a component.

  • The commodity takes over

    Blue is for links and entry points. Inside a commodity, its colour drives the share bars, chart lines and the trade ticket. Decisions stay primary dark.

  • Physical feedback

    Anything that commits sits on a 4px edge and sinks into it. Buttons scale to 0.97, motion settles on a spring, and every working button shows it is working.

Hover the card for the commodity wash. Press a figure to sink it. Switch commodity in the rail.

Rules

These come from how the business works, not from taste. Every component and screen obeys them.

Exceptions are the interface

Screens answer "is anything wrong?" first. Nothing to do is a designed, confident state, not an empty one. Show changes, not levels.

Every figure is derived, and can show how

Balances, stock, exposure and margin are computed, never typed. A headline figure reveals the subtraction behind it in one interaction.

Provenance and confirmation never merge

How a weight was captured and whether the counterparty agrees are two separate chips, with two separate icons.

Absent is not zero

A figure withheld from a role is genuinely missing. It shows as a quaternary dash with eye-off, and never as K0, blank, loading or blurred.

Three outcomes, three looks

Within tolerance books. Held for review goes to the owner. A hard block, like tare above gross, never books and is shown calmly, not as an error flood.

Nothing is edited

Records are append-only. A correction is a compensating entry with a reason, an actor and a time, beside the original.

Freshness is visible, degraded modes are loud

A silent weighbridge feed makes the position look flat, so sync health is always shown, and silence is unmistakable. Offline is a state with a queue.

Every working button shows it is working

The label fades and scales to 0.8 while a spinner takes its place. The button keeps its size and stays busy until the action lands.

Status is always an icon and a word, never colour alone. Icons are Lucide only: no emoji, no Unicode symbols standing in for icons. Charts are TradingView Lightweight Charts.

Colour

Two layers. Components use only the semantic tokens; the "Points to" column shows the palette step each one resolves to. Hover mixes 5% black into light fills and 10% white into filled buttons.

Palettes

Eleven steps each. The brand ramp keeps the reference's lightness per step, with One Link blue at 500.

Commodity colours

The deep shade carries a white label at AA or better. Underlines are 2px, washes are 9% in oklab, tiles 16%.

Type

Two families. Inter for everything you read. Instrument Sans condensed, always uppercase, for headline figures, commodity tabs and detail titles. Specs are size/line height · weight · tracking.

Regular is 480

A touch heavier than 400, which keeps small translucent text crisp on white. Strong body is 580, strong headings 600.

14px carries the product

body-3 is the workhorse for tables, meta lines and rows. Headings are rare; condensed figures do the shouting.

Every figure is tabular

Money, tonnes, percentages, refs and timestamps all use tabular numerals. Kwacha millions take M: K125M, K174,840.

Shape

A radius scale with one odd step, 14px, used only on 48px buttons. On white, shadows do the separating that grey fills would do elsewhere.

Elevation

Six exact shadow stacks.

Motion

Springs are CSS linear() curves, so there is no animation library. Every animation has a reduced-motion fallback that keeps colour changes and drops scale, translate and ping.

Components

These are the real components from project/components, running live. Every one is interactive; the commodity switch in the rail recolours the ones that take a commodity.

Buttons

Brand for entry points, primary for decisions, critical for destructive, commodity only on the trade ticket. Every button that starts work has the loading state.

Press buttons

A face on a 4px edge. It sinks 4px in 50ms and returns in 100ms. Filled edges are the fill under 15% black.

Capsules, segmented, stepper, tabs, search

Selected capsules go white with a 1px ring and a 3px halo; the label weight settles over 180ms. Tab underlines glide on the spring.

Status, trust and sync

Status marks are an icon and a word. Provenance and confirmation are separate chips. Sync health is green while live and red, without a ping, when silent.

Cards, figures and rail rows

List items carry share bars in the commodity colour, quantity chips and figure buttons. Figures open their derivation.

Data table

Dense, tabular, with ref links, a restricted cell, a derived cell and a correction row. Tables scroll inside their own container on phones.

Charts

TradingView Lightweight Charts, styled to the system: dotted grid, magnet crosshair, end-of-line labels, dashed policy lines. The TradingView logo stays visible.
